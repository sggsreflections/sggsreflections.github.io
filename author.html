Academic & Professional Background

Prof. Devinder Singh Sekhon holds a doctorate in Chemistry and Educational Administration. Throughout his professional career, he taught at both the college and university levels in India and Canada, which afforded him the scholarly discipline and methodical rigor necessary for sacred scriptural research.

Early Inspiration & Literary Beginnings

His interest in Sikh history and scriptures was kindled in elementary school through the renowned "Seetal" series by S. Sohan Singh Seetal, which depicted the selfless sacrifices of Sikh martyrs. He began writing short stories and poetry during his college days at Qadian, Gurdaspur. In the early 1980s, his religious articles and essays were published widely across newspapers and magazines in Canada and North America.

Four Decades of Scriptural Devotion

In 1992, Prof. Sekhon began a deep and systematic study of Sri Guru Granth Sahib using the definitive pothis of Principal Sahib Singh Ji. Devoting dedicated time every day, his study frequency accelerated from 2–3 times a year to 5–6 complete readings annually.

On the suggestion of his children, he authored his first English book on the life of Guru Nanak Sahib to engage children raised in the Western world. Published and distributed freely by a multi-faith organization in Edmonton, Alberta, it gained immense affection. His subsequent work, The Divine Message of Guru Granth Sahib, covering 17 popular topics, earned critical acclaim across academic and religious circles.

Published Literary Contributions (9 Books)

  • Life of Guru Nanak Sahib (English) – Published in Edmonton, Alberta, Canada and distributed free across multi-faith communities.
  • The Divine Message of Guru Granth Sahib – Covering 17 fundamental scriptural topics.
  • Shiromani Gurdwara Parbandhak Committee (SGPC) Publication – Recognized and published by SGPC Amritsar.
  • Bhai Chatar Singh Jivan Singh Publications – Three books published by the historic Amritsar publishing house.
  • Two Books Under SGPC Evaluation – Awaiting evaluation and publication.
  • Gurbani Sandesh Website – Founder and manager of www.gurbanisandesh.com, reaching global seekers.

The Sri Guru Granth Sahib Translation Project

Following requests from close friends and readers for an authentic, faithful English translation of Sri Guru Granth Sahib, Prof. Sekhon undertook this momentous task on 29.01.2017 after prayer (Ardas) to Guru Nanak Dev Ji.

Throughout this demanding six-year endeavor, he worked meticulously to ensure objective fidelity, keeping personal biases out and rigorously verifying every interpretation against the fundamental principles of Gurbani.

Author's Request to Kind-Hearted Readers

"Reviewing the holy books was a much more difficult task than I had expected because of many components to each and every holy Shabad. Rather than cursing me for any mistakes, I will be highly thankful if you kind-hearted folks pointed out the mistakes and made suggestions for improvement. It has to be a concerted effort to inculcate love and reverence for the holy Gurbani among our younger generation."

Please send your valuable feedback and corrections directly to: sekhonds@hotmail.ca
