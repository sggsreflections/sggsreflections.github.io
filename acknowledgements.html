HERTFELT THANKS

Acknowledgements • ਦਿਲੀ ਧੰਨਵਾਦ

Myriads of Thanks to You, oh Satguru Nanak, and Waheguru, who showered me with enormous kindness to complete this Great task of translating the Divine Bani into English.

There were many challenging situations during this very tough task, but You enlightened me and showed me the right path. At the same time, it was highly satisfying and blissful. You also protected me from my personal biases. I cannot thank You enough. It was purely Your kindness which sustained my efforts.

Waheguru, Waheguru, Waheguru, Waheguru, Waheguru
Dhunn Guru Nanak, Dhunn Guru Nanak, Dhunn Guru Nanak.
Slave of Your slaves
Devinder Singh Sekhon
Canada.
e-mail: SEKHONDS@HOTMAIL.CA
March May 17, 2024.
